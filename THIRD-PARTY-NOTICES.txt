Playback and additional image formats use libmpv and its linked libraries
(FFmpeg, libplacebo, libass and others). The public source build defaults to
zhongfly's explicitly designated LGPL libmpv build, not a GPL libmpv build.
mpv uses the LGPL-2.1-or-later option; this build links to LGPLv3 FFmpeg,
so the combined playback library is covered by LGPL-3.0-or-later.
PV calls the replaceable libmpv-2.dll through the public dynamic-library API.
PV does not modify libmpv or its linked libraries.
